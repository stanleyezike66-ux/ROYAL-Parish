History of St. Peter the Apostle Parish

St. Peter the Apostle Parish, Four Corners, Ikom, is a Catholic community of faith within the Catholic Diocese of Ogoja, Cross River State, Nigeria.

The parish bears the name of Saint Peter the Apostle, one of the twelve Apostles of Jesus Christ and one of the central figures in the early Christian Church.

Saint Peter was called by Christ to follow Him and was entrusted with an important responsibility in the life and mission of the Church. His life of faith, leadership, service and perseverance continues to inspire Christians throughout the world.

The Catholic Faith in Ogoja and Ikom

The history of St. Peter the Apostle Parish forms part of the wider history of Catholic evangelization in Cross River State.

Catholic missionary activity in the region developed during the early twentieth century. In 1921, Catholic missionaries arrived in present-day Ogoja and established a missionary centre from which evangelization spread to surrounding communities.

The Apostolic Prefecture of Ogoja was established in 1938 and was later elevated to the Diocese of Ogoja on 1 January 1955.

St. Peter the Apostle Parish, Ikom

St. Peter the Apostle Parish is located at Four Corners, Ikom, along Ogoja Road. The parish serves as a place of worship, evangelization, Christian formation and community life for Catholics in Ikom and surrounding communities.

The parish community gathers to celebrate Holy Mass, receive the Sacraments, participate in Catholic devotions and ministries, educate children in the faith and serve God and their neighbours.

Over the years, the parish community has continued to grow through the dedication of priests, religious, catechists, lay faithful, families, youth and various parish organizations.

Our Patron — Saint Peter

Saint Peter, originally called Simon, was one of the twelve Apostles of Jesus Christ. He was a fisherman from Galilee before being called by Jesus to become His disciple.

Jesus gave Simon the name Peter, meaning "rock", and entrusted him with a special responsibility among the Apostles.

After the Resurrection and Ascension of Jesus, Peter became an important leader of the early Christian community. The Acts of the Apostles records his preaching and leadership in the early Church.

According to Christian tradition, Peter eventually went to Rome and was martyred during the persecution of Christians.

The Catholic Church traditionally recognizes Saint Peter as its first Pope.

Benedict Mose, a Catholic priest who has been privileged to serve God and His people for the past 14 years in the priesthood. My journey to the priesthood began with a deep sense of God's call to serve Him and His Church. I responded to this call by entering the seminary, where I spent approximately 10 years of formation. Those years were a significant period of spiritual, intellectual, human, and pastoral formation. Through prayer, studies, discipline, community life, and pastoral experiences, I was prepared for the sacred ministry of the priesthood. After completing my seminary formation, I was ordained a Catholic priest. Since my ordination, I have spent 14 years serving God's people, bringing the total period from the beginning of my seminary journey to my present priestly ministry to approximately 24 years. Over the years, I have had the privilege of serving in different parish communities. My pastoral journey has taken me through five parishes, including St. John the Baptist Parish, Wola; Mater Dei Parish, Otere; and currently St. Peter the Apostle Royal Parish, Four Corners, Ikom. Throughout my priestly ministry, I have always tried to make myself available to the people entrusted to my care. I consider myself a generous man, a builder, a counsellor, and, above all, a man of peace. I believe that the priest is called not only to celebrate the Sacraments and preach the Gospel but also to build people, strengthen communities, encourage the weak, reconcile those who are divided, and promote peace wherever he serves. My priesthood has been a journey of faith, service, sacrifice, learning, and gratitude. I am grateful to God for the privilege of answering His call and for the many people who have supported and accompanied me throughout my journey. As I continue my priestly ministry, my prayer is that God will continue to give me the grace, wisdom, strength, humility, and love needed to serve Him faithfully and to be a blessing to His people. To God be the glory for the past 24 years of formation and ministry, and may He continue to guide me in the years ahead. Amen.]

About our parish

St Peter Parish, Ikom, known as The Royal Parish, is a Catholic community that gathers each week to worship, learn and serve. We are named for Saint Peter, the fisherman Christ called to lead his Church.

Our parish life is built on the Mass, the sacraments, prayer and works of charity. Every parishioner, young and old, is welcome to take part.
