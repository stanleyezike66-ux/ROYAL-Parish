The sacraments

Baptism
Welcomes a person into the Church and gives new life in Christ.
Confirmation
Strengthens the baptised with the gifts of the Holy Spirit.
Holy Eucharist
The Body and Blood of Christ, the source and summit of our life.
Reconciliation
God's forgiveness and peace for sins committed after Baptism.
Anointing of the Sick
Comfort and strength for those who are seriously ill or growing old.
Holy Matrimony
The lifelong covenant of a man and a woman before God.
Holy Orders
The call to serve the Church as a deacon, priest or bishop.

To prepare for a sacrament, visit the parish office or send us a message.
